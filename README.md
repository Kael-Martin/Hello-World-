## Project Overview

This project analyzes data from 500 startup companies to identify which factors most strongly influence startup success, measured by final company valuation.

Using statistical techniques including hypothesis testing and regression analysis, the project evaluates relationships between startup characteristics and financial outcomes. The goal is to determine which variables best predict higher company valuations.

---

## Research Objective

The primary research question for this project is:

**Which variables have the greatest impact on a startup company's final valuation?**

---

---

## Results

The analysis indicates that **initial funding amount** has the strongest positive relationship with final company valuation.

While several variables were examined, early-stage funding consistently showed the highest correlation with company success compared to other factors.

**Conclusion:**  
Startups with higher initial funding levels are significantly more likely to achieve higher valuations.
